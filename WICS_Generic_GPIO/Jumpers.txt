=================================================================
JUMPER SETTINGS
On the Generic GPIO PCB following jumpers exist:
=================================================================
Main-A PCB: JP7 = 5V ON or OFF on V+ Line on the I2C Bus
=================================================================
Main-B PCB: JP1 = 5V ON or OFF on V+ Line on the I2C Bus
=================================================================
MCP23017 PCB: JP5/JP8, JP6/JP9, JP7/JP10 = MCP23017 Addresses.
              J3/J7    J4/J6             = IO-Signal functions.
              Each signal has 2-jumpers, X1-X2, Y1-Y2
              JP1/JP3  JP2/JP4           = Set Pull-Up or Pull-Down
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Address Jumpers:
A0  A1  A2    Address
GND GND GND = 0x20
3V3 GND GND = 0x21
GND 3V3 GND = 0x22
GND GND 3V3 = 0x23
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Signal Jumpers:
X1 - X2 OFF = Output through limiting resister (LED). 
Y1 - Y2 OFF
---------------------------------------------------------------
X1 - X2 OFF  = Direct Connection (no resistor).
Y1 - Y2 ON 
-----------------------------------------------------------------
X1 - X2 ON  = Pull-Up or Down (protected by limiting resistor).
Y1 - Y2 OFF                   (If a short should happen) 
-----------------------------------------------------------------
X1 - X2 ON  = Pull-Up or Down direct connection.
Y1 - Y2 ON 
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Pull-Up/Pull-Down jumpers:
PIN1-PIN2 = Pull Down to GND
PIN2-PIN3 = Pull-Up to 3V3
-----------------------------------------------------------------
